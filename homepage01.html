ABOUT EARTHQUAKE SAFETY

Preparedness Can Save Lives.

Earthquakes can happen suddenly and cause seriousdamage to buildings, communities and essential services. During these emergencies, vulnerableindividuals may face additional difficultiesunderstanding warnings, moving to safer areasand receiving assistance.

This website provides practical earthquake safety information to help individuals, caregivers and communities understand potential risks and prepare for emergencies. Our goal is to encourage better preparedness and promote the safety of everyone.

Why Preparation Matters

Preparing before an earthquake helps people understand what to do, where to go and howto respond during unexpected situations.

Protecting Vulnerable People

Older adults, children and people with disabilities may need additional assistance during evacuation and emergency situations.

Building Safer Communities

Sharing reliable information and encouraging emergency planning can improve community preparedness and cooperation.

Three Important Stages of Earthquake Safety

Understanding what to do before, during and afteran earthquake can help reduce risks and improve safety.

01.

Before an Earthquake

Prepare emergency supplies, identify safeareas, secure heavy furniture and createan emergency communication plan.
