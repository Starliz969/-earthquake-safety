Earthquake Safety for Children

Learn simple and important ways to stay safe before, during and after an earthquake. Being prepared can help children protect themselves and others.

Welcome, Young Learners!

Earthquakes can happen suddenly and make the ground shake. Knowing what to do can help us stay calm and safe. Let's learn some important earthquake safety rules!

Important Earthquake Safety Tips

Use the arrows to explore these important safety tips.

1. Prepare an Emergency Kit

Keep an emergency bag ready with drinking water, snacks, a flashlight, a first-aid kit and other important supplies. Make sure you know where it is.

2. Learn Safe Places

Find safe places at home and school. Choose a sturdy table or desk where you can take cover and stay away from windows.

3. Listen to Adults

Pay attention to teachers, parents and caregivers. They can help you understand safety instructions and know what to do during an emergency.

4. Drop, Cover and Hold On

When shaking begins, drop to your hands and knees, cover your head and neck under sturdy furniture if available, and hold on until the shaking stops.

5. Stay Away from Windows

Stay away from windows, glass, mirrors and heavy objects that could fall. These objects may become dangerous during an earthquake.

6. Stay Calm and Move Carefully

Do not run or rush while the ground is shaking. Protect yourself first and wait until the shaking stops before moving to a safer place.

7. Stay with a Trusted Adult

After the shaking stops, stay with your teacher, parent or caregiver. Do not wander away or enter damaged buildings.

8. Be Ready for Aftershocks

Smaller earthquakes called aftershocks may happen after the main earthquake. Follow adult instructions and be ready to protect yourself again.

Earthquake Safety Guide

Before an Earthquake

How to Prepare

  • Learn safe places at home and school.
  • Prepare an emergency kit with necessary supplies.
  • Secure heavy furniture with adult help.
  • Practise earthquake safety drills with teachers and family.
  • Know how to contact your family during an emergency.
During an Earthquake

How to Protect Yourself

  • Drop to your hands and knees.
  • Cover your head and neck under sturdy furniture if possible.
  • Hold on until the shaking stops.
  • Stay away from windows and glass.
  • If you are outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings and power lines.
  • Listen to teachers and caregivers.
After an Earthquake

What to Do Next

  • Stay with your teacher, parent or caregiver.
  • Watch out for broken glass and damaged areas.
  • Be prepared for aftershocks.
  • Follow instructions from trusted adults.
  • Do not enter damaged buildings.
  • Ask an adult for help if someone is injured.
Important Safety Reminders

Always Remember!

  • Stay calm and avoid panicking.
  • Never use lifts during an earthquake.
  • Do not run outside while the ground is shaking.
  • Never touch fallen electrical wires.
  • Always follow official safety instructions.
