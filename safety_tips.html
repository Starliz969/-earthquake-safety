Earthquake Safety Tips

Learn how to stay safe and protect others before, during and after an earthquake!

01. General Earthquake Safety

Essential safety guidelines for everyone!

Before an Earthquake

  • Prepare an emergency kit with essential supplies!
  • Identify safe places inside your building!
  • Secure heavy furniture and objects!
  • Keep emergency contact numbers nearby!
  • Learn evacuation routes and safety procedures!
Learn More →

During an Earthquake

  • Drop, Cover and Hold On!
  • Stay away from windows and glass!
  • Protect your head and neck!
  • Do not use elevators!
  • Stay indoors until shaking stops!
Learn More →

After an Earthquake

  • Check yourself and others for injuries!
  • Be prepared for aftershocks!
  • Leave damaged buildings when it is safe!
  • Listen to official emergency announcements!
  • Avoid damaged buildings and structures!
Learn More →

02. Special Safety Guidance

Additional safety measures for people who may need extra support!

Older Adults

  • Keep walking aids, glasses and hearing aids accessible!
  • Prepare essential medications and medical information!
  • Identify safe places that are easy to reach!
  • Ask a trusted person for assistance if needed!
  • Keep emergency supplies within easy reach!

People with Disabilities

  • Prepare an emergency plan based on individual needs!
  • Keep mobility and communication devices nearby!
  • Identify accessible safe areas!
  • Inform trusted people about the assistance you may need!
  • Keep backup batteries for essential devices!

Children

  • Learn how to Drop, Cover and Hold On!
  • Understand emergency procedures!
  • Keep important contact information available!
  • Stay with trusted adults during emergencies!
  • Know where to meet family members after an earthquake!

People with Limited Mobility

  • Identify safe places that can be reached easily!
  • Keep mobility aids within reach!
  • Prepare an assistance plan with trusted people!
  • Keep emergency supplies nearby!
  • Practise an accessible emergency plan!

03. Caregiver Guidelines

Learn how caregivers can protect and support vulnerable people!

Before an Earthquake

  • Understand each person's medical and mobility needs!
  • Prepare emergency supplies and medications!
  • Identify accessible safe areas!
  • Make an emergency communication plan!
  • Practise safety procedures together!

During an Earthquake

  • Give calm and clear instructions!
  • Help people reach a safe position if possible!
  • Protect those who need physical assistance!
  • Keep mobility aids nearby!
  • Follow Drop, Cover and Hold On procedures!

After an Earthquake

  • Check everyone's condition and safety!
  • Provide necessary assistance!
  • Help people move away from damaged areas!
  • Check essential medications and supplies!
  • Provide emotional support and reassurance!
