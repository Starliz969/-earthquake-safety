Before an Earthquake

Prepare Today to Stay Safer Tomorrow.

1. Why Should We Prepare?

Earthquakes can happen without warning. Preparing in advance helps us respond more safely and reduces confusion during an emergency.

Everyone should know where to find emergency supplies, how to contact family members, and what to do when the ground starts shaking.

2. Important Preparation Steps

Follow these important steps to prepare your home or care centre.

01

Prepare an Emergency Kit

Prepare drinking water, food, a flashlight, a first-aid kit, batteries, and important personal supplies.

02

Secure Heavy Furniture

Secure tall shelves, cabinets, and heavy furniture to help prevent them from falling during an earthquake.

03

Identify Safe Places

Identify sturdy tables or desks where people can take cover. Stay away from windows and heavy objects.

04

Make an Emergency Plan

Discuss what everyone should do, where to meet, and how to contact family members after an earthquake.

05

Keep Exits Clear

Keep corridors, doorways, and stairsfree from objects that could block people from moving safely.

06

Practise Safety Drills

Practise Drop, Cover and Hold On so everyone understands what to do when an earthquake happens.

3. Prepare an Emergency Kit

An emergency kit contains important supplies that people may need after an earthquake.

  • Drinking water
  • Non-perishable food
  • First-aid supplies
  • Flashlight and extra batteries
  • Whistle
  • Important medicines
  • Copies of important documents
  • Mobile phone and power bank
  • Extra clothes and hygiene supplies

4. More Preparation Tips

Open each section to learn more about earthquake preparation.

  • Keep walking aids and wheelchairs accessible.
  • Prepare extra medicines and medical supplies.
  • Keep glasses and hearing aids in an easy-to-reach place.
  • Make sure emergency instructions are easy to understand.
  • Arrange helpers for people who need extra support.

  • Teach children basic earthquake safety rules.
  • Explain where safe places are located.
  • Practise earthquake drills together.
  • Make sure children know who to contact in an emergency.

  • Prepare an individual emergency support plan.
  • Keep mobility and communication aids accessible.
  • Use visual or written instructions when needed.
  • Identify trusted people who can provide assistance.

  • Secure heavy furniture and equipment.
  • Keep emergency exits clear.
  • Identify safe areas in each room.
  • Check emergency supplies regularly.
  • Make sure everyone knows the emergency plan.
