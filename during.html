During an Earthquake

Stay Calm and Protect Yourself When The Ground Starts Shaking.

1. Drop , Cover and Hold On

Follow these three important steps during earthquake shaking.

DROP

Get Down On Your Hands And Knees

COVER

Protect Your Head And Neck Under A Sturdy Table Or Desk.

HOLD ON

Hold Onto Your Shelter Until The Shaking Stops.

DROP
When you feel an earthquake starting, immediately get down on your hands and knees to prevent yourself from being knocked over by the strong shaking. This position helps you maintain your balance and keeps you closer to the ground, reducing the risk of falling and getting injured. Avoid standing, walking, or running around because the shaking can make it difficult to move safely. Once you are down, crawl carefully towards a nearby sturdy table or desk if one is available. Stay low and prepare to protect your head and neck from falling objects or debris. This is the first step of the Drop, Cover, and Hold On safety method.
COVER
Once you are down on your hands and knees, protect your head and neck by covering them with your arms to reduce the risk of injury from falling objects or debris. If possible, crawl underneath a sturdy table or desk to create a protective barrier between yourself and anything that might fall. Hold your position and keep away from windows, glass doors, shelves, hanging decorations, and heavy furniture that could move or fall during the earthquake. If there is no table or desk nearby, move towards an interior wall away from windows and use both arms to protect your head and neck. Remember to stay low and remain under cover until the shaking completely stops.
HOLD ON
Once you are safely under a sturdy table or desk, hold onto it firmly with one hand to keep yourself protected. If the shelter moves or shifts because of the earthquake, be prepared to move along with it so you remain covered. Use your other hand to continue protecting your head and neck from falling objects or debris. Try to stay as calm as possible and avoid moving away from your shelter while the ground is still shaking. Remain in this position until the shaking completely stops. After the earthquake has ended, carefully check your surroundings for possible dangers before moving to a safer location.

2. Safety in Different Places

Select a place to learn what you should do during an earthquake.

Indoors

  • Stay inside while the shaking is happening.
  • Take cover under a sturdy table or desk.
  • Stay away from windows and glass objects.
  • Protect your head and neck.

Outdoors

  • Move to an open area if you can do so safely.
  • Stay away from buildings, trees and power lines.
  • Protect your head and neck.
  • Stay in the open area until the shaking stops.

In Flats

  • Do not use lifts during the shaking.
  • Stay away from windows and balconies.
  • Take cover where you are instead of rushing down the stairs.
  • Follow Drop, Cover and Hold On.
