After an Earthquake

Stay Alert, Check for Safety and Support Others.

1. What Should You Do After an Earthquake?

When the shaking stops, it is important to remain calmand check your surroundings. Buildings, roads, andother structures may have been damaged.

Remember that aftershocks may happen, so continue following safety instructions and stay alert.

2. Important Safety Steps

Follow these steps after an earthquake to protect yourself and others.

01

Check for Injuries

Check yourself and others for injuries. Give basic first aid if you are trained and contact emergency services when needed.

02

Watch for Hazards

Stay away from broken glass, fallen objects, damaged buildings, and exposed electrical wires.

03

Prepare for Aftershocks

Smaller earthquakes may follow the main earthquake. Be ready to Drop, Cover and Hold On.

04

Listen to Official Updates

Follow instructions and updates from emergency services and local authorities.

05

Contact Your Family

Let your family members know you are safe. Use text messages when phone networks are busy.

06

Stay in a Safe Area

Follow official advice about whetherto remain where you are or move to a designated safe location.

3. Supporting Vulnerable People

Some people may need additional support after an earthquake. Help them access essential supplies and communicate with their families.

Older Adults

  • Check their physical condition and wellbeing.
  • Help them access necessary medicines.
  • Provide drinking water, food, and hygiene supplies.
  • Help them contact family members.

People with Disabilities

  • Provide assistance based on their individual needs.
  • Help them access wheelchairs and mobility aids.
  • Use clear communication and accessible instructions.
  • Make sure their essential supplies are available.

Children

  • Keep children with trusted adults.
  • Explain the situation using simple words.
  • Provide food, water, and comfort.
  • Help them contact their families.

Care Centre Staff

  • Check all residents and account for everyone.
  • Report injuries and urgent needs.
  • Check emergency supplies and medicines.
  • Provide clear updates to residents and families.

4. More Information

Open each section to learn more about staying safe after an earthquake.

Aftershocks are smaller earthquakes that happen after a larger earthquake. They can cause additional damage to buildings that are already weakened.
  • Stay alert for more shaking.
  • Be ready to Drop, Cover and Hold On.
  • Stay away from damaged buildings.

  • Do not enter damaged buildings.
  • Do not touch fallen electrical wires.
  • Do not use lifts in damaged buildings.
  • Do not spread unverified information.
  • Do not ignore official safety instructions.

  • Use your prepared emergency kit.
  • Check available drinking water and food.
  • Contact local emergency support organizations.
  • Keep important medicines and documents accessible.

  • Check on neighbours who may need assistance.
  • Share accurate emergency information.
  • Help distribute essential supplies through organized support.
  • Follow instructions from trained emergency workers.
