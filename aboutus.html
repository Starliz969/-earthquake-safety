Our Purpose

Our main goal is to improve earthquake preparedness in care centres by providing accessible and easy-to-understand safety information for staff, older adults, children and people with disabilities.

  • Raise awareness about earthquake risks and safety.
  • Share simple instructions for what to do before, during and after an earthquake.
  • Encourage caregivers to prepare emergency supplies and safety plans.
  • Promote accessible safety information for people with different needs.
  • Encourage teamwork and cooperation during emergencies.

  • Care Centre Staff: Helping caregivers understand emergency procedures and support residents.
  • Older Adults: Providing clear guidance for residents who may need additional assistance.
  • Children: Teaching basic earthquake safety through simple and visual resources.
  • People with Disabilities: Promoting accessible instructions and individual emergency support.

We plan to learn about the needs and existing earthquake safety practices of care centres. We will then share useful information through presentations, demonstrations, illustrated guides, posters and educational videos.

We hope our project will help create a more prepared and informed care centre community, where residents and caregivers understand how to respond to earthquakes and look out for one another.

Helping vulerable communities stay safe through earthquake preparedness, awareness , and emergency support.
