Myanmar's Earthquake History

Myanmar is located in a seismically active region, meaning earthquakes can occur because of movements along active faults.

According to the World Bank, 12 earthquakes of magnitude 6.0 or higher impacted Myanmar during the 21st century.

Did You Know?

Myanmar has experienced several destructive earthquakes throughout its history. The 2025 earthquake was one of the strongest recorded events to affect the country.

The figure of 12 counts earthquakes of magnitude 6 or higher during the 21st century. It does not represent every small earthquake in Myanmar.

Areas Affected by the 2025 Earthquake

On 28 March 2025, a magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck central Myanmar. Its epicentre was located near Sagaing and Mandalay.

The earthquake also affected several other areas across the country.

Sagaing Region

Near the earthquake's epicentre and among the hardest-hit areas.

Mandalay Region

Experienced severe building damage and casualties

Nay Pyi Taw

Buildings and important facilities were damaged

Southern Shan State

Towns including Kalaw and Nyaungshwe were affected.

Bago Region

Also experienced earthquake impacts and infrastructure damage.

Magway Region

Included among the affected regions.

Why Were These Places Affected?

The earthquake produced strong shaking across central Myanmar, damaging buildings, roads, bridges and other structures.

Human Impact

The 2025 earthquake caused a major humanitariancrisis. Many people lost family members, homes and access to essential services.

Other challenges faced by survivors:
  • Lack of safe shelter and clean water.
  • Limited access to hospitals and medicine.
  • Loss of family members and loved ones.
  • Stress and emotional difficulties.
  • Disruption to education and daily life.

Community Support and Solidarity

During the earthquake response, peoplesupported one another through rescue work, donations and emergency assistance.

Search and Rescue

Local volunteers and rescue teams helped search for people trapped in damaged buildings.

Food and Water

Communities and aid groups distributed food, drinking water and essential supplies.

Medical Assistance

Medical teams and volunteers supported injured people and helped them receive treatment.

Temporary Shelter

People helped affected families find temporary places to stay and provided shelter materials.

Donations

People contributed money, clothes, food and other essential items.

Supporting Vulnerable People

Families and volunteers helped older adults, children and people with disabilities access support.

Community impact

Cooperation between local communities, volunteers and rescue organizations helped provide important support during the emergency.
